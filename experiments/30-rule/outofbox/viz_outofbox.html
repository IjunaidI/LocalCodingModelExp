The thread

Repair tried to fix the monolith; voting tried to outvote it; decomposition simply never built it. Shrink the unit of generation until it's both easy and checkable.

A full 30-rule attempt is a knife-edge that collapses under sampling — so voting has no committee. An atomic one-rule attempt is rock-solid and unit-testable — so decomposition turns the impossible monolith into a reliable 23/23 pipeline. The lone miss was the same old trap in miniature: an ambiguous “1000/900” shorthand, and the empty-cell boolean.
